FileMend

File too big for the site?

FileMend makes photos, PDFs and videos fit any upload limit — 100 KB, 1 MB, 2 MB or whatever the site asks for. Everything happens on your phone, nothing is uploaded, and your original stays untouched.

How it works

  • Choose a photo, PDF or video — or tap “Share → FileMend” in any app.
  • Pick the size the site allows, or paste the site's error message.
  • Check the result and send or save it.
